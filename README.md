Cristofell Pizzaria Chef App

A mobile-first Expo and React Native application that helps the chef manage the restaurant menu and daily operations.

Requirement upgrades based on the MAST POE brief.


- Create, edit, search, filter, preview, and delete menu items
- Review menu totals, average pricing, and course statistics
- Manage kitchen orders and assign chefs
- Track inventory levels, storage locations, and review dates
- Maintain staff attendance, weekly tasks, and calendar assignments
- Review recipes, food costs, sales, expenses, and safety checks
- Search dishes and staff from one place


 Login validation

Enter a valid email address and a password containing at least six characters. This prototype validates the form locally and does not connect to an authentication service.
